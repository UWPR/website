*UWPR's state of the art instrumentation and equipment*

[![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/B59_01.jpg)](#)

The Proteomics Resource's instrument room is located in the basement of 850 Republican St. in ~3500 sq. ft. of lab space.

## Instruments

The facility currently houses six mass spectrometers for use by UWPR collaborators:

- [Astral]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/astral/)
- [Orbitrap Fusion Lumos Tribrid]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/orbitrapfusionlumos/)
- [Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/orbitrapfusion/)
- [Orbitrap Exploris480 1 and 2]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/orbitrapexploris480/)
- [TSQ Altis]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/tsqaltis/)

The facility also offers two types of nano-liquid chromatography and autosampler instruments:

- [EASY-nLC™ 1200]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/EASYnLC/) (qty. 6, for TSQ Altis, Tribrids, and Exploris 480)
- [Vanquish Neo]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/VanquishNeo/) (qty. 2, for Astral and Exploris 480 only)

The full list, including retired instruments, is on the [Instruments page]({{ site.baseurl }}/facility/instruments/).
