*A compact nano-flow UHPLC system*

The EASY-nLC combines simplicity and high-throughput capacity in a nano-flow UHPLC with a pressure rating of 1200 bar.

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/instruments/TSQAltis.jpg)

## System specifications {#specifications}

| Specification | Value |
|:--|:--|
| Pressure range | 0 – 1200 bar |
| Flow range, gradient | 20 – 2000 nL/min (recommended 100 – 1000 nL/min) |
| Flow range, loading & equilibration | up to 25 µL/min |
| Retention time reproducibility | 0.1 – 0.4% RSD within recommended flow range |
| Pick-up volume range | 0.10 – 18.00 µL (20 µL loop) |
| Injection reproducibility | ≤ 0.2% RSD at 5 µL pick-up; ≤ 3.0% RSD at 0.1 µL pick-up |
| Injection linearity | R ≥ 0.9985 for 0.5 – 10 µL; R ≥ 0.9995 for 0.3 – 1.6 µL |
| Carry-over | < 0.05% (caffeine) |
| Gradient delay volume | < 1 µL (mixing tee to venting tee) |
