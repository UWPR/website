*Triple Quadrupole Mass Spectrometer*

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/instruments/TSQAltis01b.png)

The TSQ Altis is a triple quadrupole mass spectrometer with Active Ion Management (AIM™) ion optics, segmented quadrupoles, and electron multiplier detection.

TSQ Altis, Installed 10/16/2018

## Specifications {#specs}

| Specification | Value |
|:--|:--|
| Mass range | m/z 5 – 2,000 |
| SRM rate | 600 SRMs/sec |
| Selectivity | 0.2 Da FWHM for high-resolution SRM |
| Sensitivity | 500,000 : 1 |

**Available scan functions**

- Collision induced dissociation (CID)
- 3,000 timed SRMs (T-SRM)
- QED-MS/MS (simultaneous quantitation and structural confirmation)
- iSRM (intelligent SRM)
