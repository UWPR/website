    Resources

    UWPR’s state of the art instrumentation and equipment

    The Proteomics Resource’s instrument room is located in the basement of 850 Republican St. in ~3500 sq. ft. of lab space. The facility currently houses six mass spectrometers for use by UWPR collaborators:

    • Astral
    • Orbitrap Fusion Lumos Tribrid
    • Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid
    • Orbitrap Exploris480 1 and 2
    • TSQ Altis

    The facility also offers two types of nano-liquid chromatography and autosampler instruments.

    • EASY-nLC™ 1200 (qty. 6, for TSQ Altis, Tribrids, and Exploris 480)
    • Vanquish Neo (qty. 2, for Astral and Exploris 480 only)

    Additionally, the Proteomics Resource has access to dedicated servers for computation, (see the Tools page for details).
