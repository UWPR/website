    Resources

    A reference collection for shotgun (bottom-up) proteomics as we practice it at the UWPR

    These pages collect the reference material we use and share at the UWPR, following the bottom-up proteomics workflow from sample preparation and digestion through acquisition strategies and data analysis. You will find background reading in the knowledgebase, step-by-step bench protocols, part lists for the consumables our systems use, and the safety documents that govern work in our lab. The MS Laboratories & Cores section shares our hardware designs and reference material with other laboratories and core facilities.

    Knowledgebase: Shotgun Proteomics

    Protein identification and quantification strategies: DDA, DIA, targeted acquisition (SRM, PRM), and stable isotope labeling.

     Sample Preparation

    Protein extraction, separation, and enrichment.

     Protein Digestion & Peptide Fractionation

    Cleaving proteins into peptides for LC-MS: MS-compatible detergents, digestion kits and enzymes, chemical cleavage, peptide fractionation and enrichment, and peptide cleanup.

     Protocols & Methods

    Step-by-step bench protocols for sample preparation, digestion, peptide fractionation, and column preparation.

     Data Analysis

    References and local tool guides for LC-MS data analysis.

     Reagents & Consumables

    Part lists, standards, and consumables used on the UWPR systems.

     MS Laboratories & Cores

    Hardware designs and reference material from the UWPR: the NSI source, the column-packing pressure cell, and common background ions.

     Safety

    UWPR safety guidelines, chemical SOPs, and UW EH&S resources.
