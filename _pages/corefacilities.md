*Proteomics and Mass Spectrometry Facilities*

## Local Facilities {#local}

The Seattle area facilities below are available to serve your proteomics needs.  
If you provide fee-for-service proteomics work and would like to be linked from this page, [send us an email](mailto:engj@u.washington.edu).

- The **Fred Hutch Proteomics & Metabolomics facility** provides mass spectrometry and HPLC services for protein and peptide characterization, including protein identification, protein modification analysis, protein/peptide purification, and qualitative/quantitative proteomics experiments. Services are provided on a fee-for-service basis.  
  [Proteomics & Metabolomics Core](https://www.fredhutch.org/en/research/shared-resources/core-facilities/proteomics-metabolomics.html)

- The **School of Pharmacy Mass Spectrometry Center**, located in the I-court below the rotunda cafeteria at the UW, offers a full array of fee-for-service options, including small molecule assay development, structure analysis, protein adduct mapping, protein-protein interaction mapping, proteomic gel band analysis, qualitative shotgun proteomics, and label-free quantitative proteomics.  
  <https://sop.washington.edu/department-of-medicinal-chemistry/mass-spectrometry-center/>

- The **Department of Chemistry Mass Spectrometry Facility**, located on the ground floor of Bagley Hall in rooms 087 and 083, provides a wide variety of analytical services to the department, university, and community, ranging from qualitative and unknown sample analysis to quantitative assays. The facility offers hands-on training and walk-up use of most instruments, providing education in many aspects of mass spectrometry.  
  <https://chem.washington.edu/facilities/mass-spectrometry-facility>

- The **Northwest Metabolomics Research Center (NW-MRC)**, located at the University of Washington School of Medicine, South Lake Union (SLU), provides a broad range of metabolomics services, including sample analysis, data analysis and interpretation, training, and workshops through its specialty cores: Mass Spectrometry, NMR and Metabolic Imaging, Biostatistics & Bioinformatics, and Promotion & Outreach.  
  [Northwest Metabolomics Research Center](https://depts.washington.edu/nwmrc/)

- The **Quantitative and Functional Proteomics Core**, located at the University of Washington School of Medicine, South Lake Union (SLU), uses UWPR instrumentation to provide proteomics services to Diabetes Research Center affiliate investigators.  
  [Diabetes Research Center Proteomics and Bioinformatics Core](https://depts.washington.edu/diabetes/proteomics-and-bioinformatics/)
