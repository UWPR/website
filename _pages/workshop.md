title: "Proteomics Workshop
            
            

      Sponsored by the UWPR Team!

      How can proteomics help your research?
      Get a basic understanding of a typical proteomics workflow.
      Get to know the UWPR personnel and resources available to you.

       
      
      Where:  South Lake Union campus
      Who: Any member of the UW
      When:  July 9th - 11th, 10 am - 3 pm
      

      
       Participants (UW members) will get an introduction to proteomics science and technologies through hands-on exploration of typical proteomics workflows. 
       This course is appropriate for individuals who have little to no experience in proteomics and desire an overview of the typical proteomics workflows. Individuals who may benefit from this course include investigators who are considering incorporating proteomics protocols into their own research, graduate students and post-doctoral fellows and prospective UW Proteomics Resource users. 
        The course is sponsored by the UWPR and is offered to UW members at no cost.

**Our July workshop is full!! If you would like to be on the waiting list (in case someone cancels) and/or be added to the mailing list for future workshops please follow the instructions below.**

Number of participants is limited to maximum of 12, so register early. We require a minimum of 6 participants for the course to take place.

**Prerequisites**

Prerequisite for participation in the course is interest in proteomics, a basic understanding of protein chemistry and a commitment to attend the entire course.

To Register please fill out the form below and e-mail it to priska@uw.edu.
