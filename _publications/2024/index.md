Support for the publications involves either instrument access, computational analysis, 
consultation with lab or computational staff, etc. It is our policy that key collaborator(s) 
within the UWPR share in co-authorship of any manuscripts that are the result of work 
performed at the Resource. Minimally, the Proteomics Resource should always be acknowledged 
as follows in publications: 

***This work is supported in part by the University of Washington's 
Proteomics Resource (UWPR95794).***
