## Design and features

The UWPR nanospray source was first designed in 2007 to be used on Thermo Fisher instruments (LTQ, LTQ-Orbitrap XL, and TSQ). Over the years the flexible design has been modified, and today it fits all the Thermo instruments, including the Exploris series, the Astral series, and the Tribrid series.

The UWPR NSI source offers all the benefits and features of commercial NSI sources:

- Lower flow rates: no drying gas or thermal heating is required, and sensitivity is increased compared to higher flow ESI
- Higher tolerance to a wide variety of liquid compositions than conventional ESI

In addition, this NSI source offers significant improvements over most commercial sources:

- Great flexibility: it accommodates columns of all lengths, in-house packed or commercial columns, as well as configurations with traps, spray tips, etc.
- The source can be customized to your specific needs, e.g. mounting of cameras, LED lights, or anything else you can think of
- It can be used in conjunction with the Thermo NSI probes (static, dynamic, and packed tip probes)
- The open design allows leaks to be detected immediately
- Significantly lower cost: the parts list and assembly instructions are free to download, allowing you to build the NSI source at a fraction of the cost of commercial sources
- All the parts are commercially available

We currently use the NSI source on all of our Thermo instruments.

An alternative for using your own columns with the Thermo EASY-Spray source is the [Jailbreak Column Heater](https://phoenix-st.com/product/jailbreak-column-heater-for-the-thermo-easy-spray-source/) from Phoenix S&T.
