*Common background ions in positive mode ESI LC-MS*

Electrospray mass spectra almost always contain background ions that originate from solvents, buffers, plasticizers, detergents, and other laboratory materials rather than from the sample. Knowing the common ones helps you recognize contamination and avoid chasing peaks that will never match a peptide. The table below lists frequently observed singly charged background ions with their monoisotopic mass, formula, and likely origin. Another good resource is the [Contamination Search Tools at the University of Leiden](http://www.lc-ms.nl/).
