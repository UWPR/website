*Polymeric background ion series in positive mode ESI LC-MS*

Many background ions appear not as single peaks but as families of peaks separated by a characteristic repeating unit, such as polymers, salt clusters, and solvent adducts. The mass difference between neighboring peaks identifies the family. The tables below list the polysiloxane series, common repeating units, and the phthalate plasticizers frequently seen in ESI spectra. Another good resource is the [Contamination Search Tools at the University of Leiden](http://www.lc-ms.nl/).

## Polydimethylcyclosiloxanes

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/esi_background_02.png)

Polydimethylcyclosiloxanes are widespread compounds in industrial products, e.g. deodorants, and are ubiquitously present in ambient air (mass difference 74.018793).
