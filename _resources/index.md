*A reference collection for shotgun (bottom-up) proteomics as we practice it at the UWPR*

These pages collect the reference material we use and share at the UWPR, following the bottom-up proteomics workflow from sample preparation and digestion through acquisition strategies and data analysis. You will find background reading in the knowledgebase, step-by-step bench protocols, part lists for the consumables our systems use, and the safety documents that govern work in our lab. The MS Laboratories & Cores section shares our hardware designs and reference material with other laboratories and core facilities.
