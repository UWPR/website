*A reference collection for shotgun (bottom-up) proteomics as we practice it at the UWPR*

## [Knowledgebase: Shotgun Proteomics]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/knowledgebase/)

Protein identification and quantification strategies: DDA, DIA, targeted acquisition (SRM, PRM),
and stable isotope labeling.

## [Sample Preparation]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/sample-preparation/)

Protein extraction, separation and enrichment, digestion, and peptide separation.

## [Protocols & Methods]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/protocols/)

Step-by-step bench protocols and LC-MS setup procedures.

## [Data Analysis]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/data-analysis/)

References for interpreting LC-MS data, including common background ions and contaminants.

## [Reagents & Consumables]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/consumables/)

Part lists, standards, and consumables used on the UWPR systems.

## [For Laboratories & Cores]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/cores/)

Build-it-yourself hardware from the UWPR: the NSI source and the column-packing pressure cell.

## [Safety]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/safety/)

UWPR safety guidelines, chemical SOPs, and UW EH&S resources.
