## Instruments

The Proteomics Resource's instrument room is located in the basement of 850 Republican St. in \~3500 sq. ft. of lab space. The
facility currently houses six mass spectrometers for use by UWPR collaborators:

- [Astral]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources/instruments/astral/)
- [Orbitrap Fusion Lumos Tribrid]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/orbitrapfusionlumos/)
- [Orbitrap Fusion Tribrid]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/orbitrapfusion/)
- [Orbitrap Exploris480 1 and 2]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/orbitrapexploris480/)
- [TSQ Altis]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/tsqaltis/)

The facility also offers two types of nano-liquid chromatography and autosampler instruments.

- [EASY-nLC™ 1200]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/EASYnLC/) (qty. 6, for TSQ Altis, Tribrids, and Exploris 480)
- [Vanquish Neo]({{ site.baseurl }}/resources//instruments/VanquishNeo/) (qty. 2, for Astral and Exploris 480 only)

Additionally, the Proteomics Resource has access to dedicated servers for computation, (see the [Tools page]({{ site.baseurl }}/tools/) for details).

Additional details of the facility are provided in this [Word document]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/docs/UWPR_Resources_for_Grant_Writing.docx) intended for grant writing.
